Your search operations deck, one click from DBX.

DBX plugins run in a sandbox without arbitrary network or frame access. This workbench keeps the SearchDeck address and hands the console to your browser, where its own login and ES credentials stay.

Why a launcher?

A DBX plugin iframe is intentionally isolated: it cannot freely reach your intranet or embed arbitrary remote pages. SearchDeck therefore stays open in the browser you already trust; this workbench only remembers the address. A future sidecar-backed mode can bring read-only panels into DBX without weakening that boundary.
